## Tenant Isolation Logic & Module Scoping
Each merchant only sees their own store type and products. This is by design:
1. **Grocery Merchants** (`grocery`) see and update their specific store products. Brand names are now loaded and displayed next to products.
2. **Restaurant Merchants** (`restaurant`) see and update their menu items.
3. **Pharmacy Merchants** (`pharmacy`) see and update their specific pharmacy medicines.
4. **Dual Mode / Multi-business**: If a phone number is linked to multiple tenants (e.g. both a restaurant and a grocery store), they will see multiple business cards under the **"MY BUSINESS"** section on their Profile tab. Tapping on a card opens that specific business's isolated CMS context.
